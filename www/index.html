Wind Chimes

Tap the chimes. Every note belongs with every other note, so there is no wrong order.

Seed Sorting

Tap a seed, then the bowl it belongs in. A seed in the wrong bowl just hops back out.

Lighting the Lanterns

Tap a lantern and it changes, along with the ones beside it. Light them all. There is a nudge if you want one.

Tide Pools

Turn over rocks. Everything under them comes in pairs. Find the pairs; nothing minds how long it takes.

Stream Stones

The channel is in pieces. Tap a stone to turn it, and get the water from the left bank to the right.
