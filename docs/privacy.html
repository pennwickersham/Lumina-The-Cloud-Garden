Privacy Policy — Lumina: The Cloud Garden

Last updated: REPLACE WITH PUBLICATION DATE

Lumina is published by Brewster Wickersham Publications LLC. This policy covers the Lumina mobile app.

What the app collects

Nothing. Lumina has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, health information or device identifiers, and it does not send any information about you anywhere.

What is stored on your device

Your garden — plants, stardust, rest, polished stones, solved constellations and your comfort settings — is saved on the device itself and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes that data. The app provides a garden code you can copy and keep if you want to move your garden to a new device or restore it after reinstalling.

Purchases

Purchases of rare plant varieties and additional islands are handled by Google Play through RevenueCat, which manages the receipt so your purchases can be restored later. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt. It does not receive your name or email address from this app. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play and are never visible to this app or to us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

All purchases are one-time. There are no subscriptions and no recurring charges.

Children

Lumina is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Lumina is a game designed to be comfortable for people living with chronic illness. It is not a medical device, it does not provide medical advice, and it is not a treatment for any condition.
