Lumina: The Cloud Garden

A gentle sky garden that adapts to your energy.

Lumina is a small island in the sky that keeps growing whether or not you show up. Each time you open it, you say how much energy you have, and the game shapes itself around your answer: puzzles and planting on better days, rain and rest on hard ones. There are no streaks, no daily goals and no timers, and being away costs you nothing.

It was made for people living with chronic illness, pain, fatigue or migraine, and for anyone who wants a game that asks very little of them.

  • Nine islands to find, from the Crystal Caverns to the Moon Garden
  • Gentle pastimes that cannot be failed or timed out
  • Comfort settings: low light, screen warmth, softened sound, bigger buttons, left-handed layout
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Optional one-time purchases only.
